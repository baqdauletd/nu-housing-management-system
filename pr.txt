Dear Students,
Fall semester is coming, and Student Housing is looking forward to seeing you on Campus! 
If you need accommodation in the dormitory, please, read this email carefully!

- Open the application link
- Follow the instructions
- Make sure your application is complete and sent on time. 

Deadline for application is June 14th, 2024 at 23:59 Astana time
No late applications will be accepted despite any extenuating cases.


Eligibility requirements:
1. Have no registered real estate property in Astana (students, parents);
2. Being a non-Astana resident (students, parents);

ASTANA RESIDENTS ARE NOT ELIGIBLE FOR ON-CAMPUS ACCOMMODATION IN FALL 2024! WE WILL ANNOUNCE A SEPARATE APPLICATION CALL FOR ASTANA RESIDENTS ONCE THERE IS A POSSIBILITY.

Please note that students whose parents work and have a property or/and registration in Astana will be counted as Astana residents.

In order to apply please fill in the form and make sure to attach all of the required documents.

FAQ:
1) If your parents are unemployed, you should submit the Certificate of registration as unemployed issued from Egov or pension contribution report that indicates no pension funding for the last 6 months.
2) If your parents are retired, you should submit the Retirement passport/certificate.
